### Task 05.01

<img src=img/moduloVisual.png height="500">

I tried to hone in on the modulo circle algorithm and explore a creative application for it. I ended up producing a music video for one of my tracks, restricting myself to manipulating only the parameters involved in the algorithm's transformations. I reused the Blueprint Actor from Task 01.07, but extended it to integrate with the Unreal Engine setup I developed for my second-term project. I learned a great deal in the process (which took much longer than expected) and am overall quite pleased with the result.
